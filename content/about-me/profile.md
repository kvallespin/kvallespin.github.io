# Profile

![Kenneth Vallespin](/assets/ken-profile.jpg)

*Systems engineering, SATCOM, finance, and data science.*

I am Kenneth Vallespin, usually Ken. I work at the intersection of systems engineering, satellite communications, infrastructure programs, corporate finance, and applied data science.

I am currently a Systems Architect and Project Manager at Neo Space Group in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, working across SATCOM and related infrastructure programs. My professional background spans telecommunications, security, commercial aviation, space, and technical program delivery. Across those areas, I have spent more than 18 years turning complex technical and commercial requirements into delivered systems, operating models, and measurable program outcomes.

I am also completing an MBA at the Asian Institute of Management. Lately, I have been spending much of my academic and personal work on corporate finance, aviation finance, data science, simulation, and AI-assisted analysis. That mix probably explains the shape of this site: engineering notes beside finance work, public reviewers beside simulation experiments, and technical projects beside essays on design systems.

My credentials sit across several jurisdictions and professional systems. I hold the European Engineer (EUR ING) title, Professional Engineer (P.E.) licenses in five U.S. states, UK Chartered Engineer (CEng) registration, the Saudi Council of Engineers Consultant-grade qualification, and the APEC Engineer title. I am also certified as a Project Management Professional (PMP), PRINCE2 Registered Practitioner and Project Manager, Lean Six Sigma Black Belt, and Certified Scrum Master.

I stay connected to the engineering profession through active membership and volunteer work with organizations such as the Institution of Engineering and Technology (IET), the Royal Aeronautical Society (RAeS), Engineers Europe, the Saudi Council of Engineers, and the Institute of Electronics Engineers of the Philippines.

> Short version: I build, manage, evaluate, and explain technical systems. Sometimes those systems are satellite networks. Sometimes they are financial models. Sometimes they are simulations designed to pressure-test a decision before reality does.

You can reach me through [linkedin.com/in/kennethvallespin](https://www.linkedin.com/in/kennethvallespin/).
