Pax Silica Philippines: 10 Year MiroFish Simulation Seed

Use and status

This seed defines a source grounded, agent rich simulation from 2026 Q3 through 2036 Q2. It is preparation only. No simulation has been run. Upload this file to MiroFish, then use the separate prompt file.

The model must distinguish documented fact, official institution, journalism, opinion, model assumption, scenario shock, and simulated output. It must not convert proposals into signed commitments or turn MiroFish dialogue into econometric evidence.

Simulation objective

Determine whether the Philippine Pax Silica initiative produces net national and household benefit over ten years relative to a no project counterfactual, while testing economic value capture, jobs, consumer power, water and internet prices, land and housing effects, water and environmental sustainability, the 2028 election, West Philippine Sea escalation, Taiwan contingencies, Middle East energy disruption, and US trade policy.

Legal and scope interpretation

  1. Treat the Pax Silica declaration as a political and economic cooperation framework. Its verbs include affirm, encourage, intend, seek, and pursue. It does not by itself disclose a funded construction program.
  2. Treat the BCDA letter as a proposal and the US letter as a conditional acceptance. Do not assume that a final lease, exact site, operating agreement, appropriation, or construction contract exists.
  3. Separate the approximately 4,000 acre Coordination Office proposal from the wider industrial hub narrative. The source pack leaves an unresolved scope question about how much land is for the office, industrial tenants, shared infrastructure, buffers, and future expansion.
  4. Do not treat the reported 130 MLD and 3 GW estimates as official commitments. Use them as stress values until an engineering study replaces them.
  5. A 2028 administration can change executive support, review agreements, alter budgets and regulation, or seek renegotiation and withdrawal. It cannot assume that valid contracts, legislation, court orders, or compensation obligations disappear.
  6. Congress controls legislation, appropriations, taxation, and oversight. The Senate and House must be represented separately. The judiciary acts only through cases and legal remedies. BSP remains a separate, independent macro financial actor.

Quantitative state ledger

  • Economic level: Real GDP, nominal GDP in PHP and USD, GDP per capita, GNI per capita, productivity.
  • Incremental contribution: Direct value added, indirect and induced effects, import leakage, crowding out, displaced activity, cumulative discounted net benefit.
  • Employment: Construction job years, permanent direct jobs, indirect jobs, induced jobs, high skill share, local resident share, displaced jobs.
  • Prices and finance: CPI inflation, USD to PHP, policy rate response, FDI, current account, fiscal revenue, incentives, guarantees, and contingent liabilities.
  • Power: MW demand ramp, dedicated supply share, renewable share, storage, reserve margin, PHP per kWh, and 200 kWh household bill.
  • Water: Freshwater MLD, recycling share, groundwater share, irrigation shortfall, PHP per cubic meter, and 20 cubic meter household bill.
  • Internet: Retail plan price index, price per Mbps, speed, latency, service coverage, and whether open access passes infrastructure gains to households.
  • Land and housing: Land price index, rent index, worker housing gap, agricultural conversion, displacement, compensation, and post cancellation reversal.
  • Sustainability: Wastewater compliance, hazardous incident risk, carbon intensity, biodiversity exposure, subsidence, water balance, and climate resilience.
  • Strategy and legitimacy: Technology transfer, patents, local supplier share, Filipino value capture, social license, election support, WPS risk, Taiwan risk state, China retaliation, and allied support.

Required causal accounting

  • Total GDP equals the no project counterfactual plus Philippine direct value added plus indirect and induced value added plus productivity spillovers, less import leakage, displaced activity, fiscal opportunity cost, utility crowding out, and shock losses.
  • GDP per capita equals GDP divided by population. GNI per capita must add net primary income and remittance effects. Do not label GDP per capita as household income.
  • Net jobs equal construction job years converted to annual averages plus permanent direct, indirect, and induced jobs, less displaced and crowded out jobs. Never add job years to permanent headcount.
  • Power rate effect equals only socialized project related generation, grid, backup, and stranded costs divided by the relevant billed energy, plus fuel and FX pass through. Dedicated ring fenced costs stay outside household tariffs.
  • Water rate effect equals only socialized bulk supply, treatment, recycling, distribution, and stranded costs divided by billed volume, plus scarcity effects. Industrial charges must not be silently shifted to households.
  • Internet effect depends on backhaul cost, open access, last mile competition, spectrum, peering, and demand. Data centers alone do not guarantee cheaper household internet.
  • Inflation effect combines oil, LNG, power, water, food, rent, wage, FX, and logistics pass through, net of productivity and competition effects.
  • FX effect responds to FDI inflows, imported capex, energy imports, exports, profit repatriation, current account pressure, risk premium, and BSP response.

Do not generate point forecasts when capex, phasing, local procurement, operating revenue, utility contracts, land transactions, or technical studies are missing. Produce low, central, and high ranges and identify which missing input drives each range.

  8. Set enforceable local supplier, workforce, research, intellectual property, and technology transfer commitments.
  9. Set cybersecurity, trusted network, data protection, cable resilience, and incident response requirements.
  10. Provide a lawful 2028 transition and exit framework covering contracts, compensation, stranded assets, and continuing public obligations.

Benefit decision rules

  • Economic: cumulative net Philippine value added is positive versus C0 in the median case and remains tolerable in the adverse tail after fiscal and import leakage costs.
  • Income: real GDP per capita and GNI per capita improve versus C0 without a material increase in poverty or regional inequality.
  • Jobs: net permanent jobs are positive, high skill participation rises, and local job gains exceed displaced farm and small business livelihoods.
  • Consumers: attributable power and water bill increases remain ring fenced or below the approved policy threshold, and internet quality improves without sustained real price increases.
  • Sustainability: no unresolved water deficit, irrigation injury, unlawful land action, serious wastewater breach, or unacceptable biodiversity loss remains.
  • Fiscal: discounted public revenue and strategic benefits exceed incentives, infrastructure subsidies, guarantees, remediation, and stranded cost exposure.
  • Resilience: the project survives the 2028 transition and major external shocks without requiring an opaque bailout or abandoning Philippine legal safeguards.

Classify the final result as beneficial, conditionally beneficial, indeterminate, or not beneficial. A beneficial result requires all hard legal, land, water, environmental, and utility gates to pass. Report the result separately for the Philippines overall, affected Central Luzon communities, households, workers, Indigenous Peoples, government finances, and strategic resilience.
